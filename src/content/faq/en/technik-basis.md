On a statically generated frontend built with Astro, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, plus a Git-based CMS for your content. In practice: few moving parts, no plugin zoo, short load times and a far smaller attack surface than a classic database install. The full stack is listed openly on the studio page — your IT team should know what they are getting.
