question: 'Does my website have to be accessible?'
category: recht
order: 71

That depends on what you offer and how large your company is: the German Accessibility Strengthening Act has applied since June 2025 and largely exempts micro-enterprises providing services. We build to WCAG 2.2 AA as the target regardless — keyboard operation, measured contrast, semantic HTML. Whether the obligation applies to you is a legal question, and that needs someone licensed to advise.
