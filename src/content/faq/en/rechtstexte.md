question: 'Do you take care of the imprint, privacy policy and cookie banner?'
category: recht

We set the imprint and privacy pages up technically and put them in place; the wording comes from you, your lawyer or a generator — we are not allowed to give legal advice. A cookie banner is usually unnecessary on our projects because no tracking is built in by default. If an analytics tool is added later, we add consent and the privacy wording together.
