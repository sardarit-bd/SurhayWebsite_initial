---
question: 'Why does a website cost more than a site-builder subscription?'
category: preise
order: 11
---
Because you are buying two different things. In a site builder you rent a template and assemble it yourself — your time is the real price there. With us you get structure, a copy framework, design and build as a finished result that belongs to you afterwards. If your offer is simple and you enjoy doing it yourself, a site builder is the more sensible choice — and we say so in the first call.
