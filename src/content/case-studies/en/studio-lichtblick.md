Photographers don't need a website that competes with their own portfolio. Studio Lichtblick needed the opposite: a stage that steps back — and a booking flow that works at 3 a.m. just as well as in a first consultation.

## What made the difference

**The images carry it.** No decorative interface, no effect overload. Large-format galleries in WebP/AVIF, perfectly compressed — the site feels fast even though it shows hundreds of photos.

**Frictionless booking.** Pick a package, pick a date, done. What used to take five DMs and three days now happens in two minutes — even on Sundays.

**Built-in qualification.** Three targeted form questions sort inquiries by shoot type and budget. The studio now only talks to people who actually want to book.
