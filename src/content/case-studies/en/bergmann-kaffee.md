Bergmann Kaffeerösterei has been an institution in Prenzlauer Berg since 2015 — but online, it simply didn't exist. The relaunch had one clear goal: translate the feeling of standing in the shop into digital, and turn it into measurable revenue.

## What made the difference

**Subscription over single purchase.** The coffee subscription was designed as the website's centerpiece — not an afterthought. A third of all new customers now choose the subscription right away.

**Speed as a feature.** Static generation, optimized WebP images, no unnecessary JavaScript. The site loads in 0.8 seconds — and Google rewards it with top rankings for "coffee roastery Berlin".

**Systematic storytelling.** Every coffee tells its origin story. That's not an end in itself: time on site tripled, and visitors who stay longer buy more often.
