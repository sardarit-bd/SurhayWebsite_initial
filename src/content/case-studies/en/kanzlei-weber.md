Legal competence is hard to show — but easy to squander. A cluttered website full of walls of text signals the opposite of diligence. Weber & Partner wanted a website that works as precisely as the firm itself.

## What made the difference

**Structure follows client logic.** Visitors don't search for "civil law" — they search for "problems with my landlord". The new architecture leads from problem to the right lawyer in two clicks at most.

**Removing barriers.** The callback form with appointment suggestion replaces the intimidating "calling a law firm" moment. Over 60% of all inquiries now arrive through this form.

**Locally visible.** Clean technical SEO plus LocalBusiness schema: for "employment lawyer Charlottenburg", the firm now ranks on page one.
